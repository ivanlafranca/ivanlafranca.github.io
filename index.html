§01

Research

My work sits at the intersection of monetary economics and firm dynamics: how policy shocks propagate unevenly across financing channels, and what that means for who gets to innovate.

Financing & Transmission

How monetary policy reaches young, VC-financed entrants differently than it reaches bank-financed incumbents — and the consequences for the pace of creative destruction.

Firm Dynamics & Growth

Endogenous growth and Hopenhayn-style firm dynamics, with an eye toward structural change and long-run productivity.

Environmental Macro

A second strand on green preferences and climate in dynamic growth settings, alongside applied replication work in macro-climate models.
